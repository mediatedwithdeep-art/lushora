LUSHORA Room Mists are formulated with our proprietary MistLock technology which delivers up to 6–8 hours of consistent fragrance in a standard room (250 sq ft). Smaller spaces like cars or bathrooms may experience even longer lasting effects.
Yes. All LUSHORA formulations are free from harmful VOCs, parabens, phthalates, and synthetic preservatives. They are dermatologist tested and safe to use around children above 3 years and most pets. We recommend ventilating the room for 5 minutes if you have birds.
Absolutely. LUSHORA is fabric-safe and non-staining. You can mist it on curtains, bed linens, sofa upholstery, and even clothing. We recommend a test spray on a small hidden area first for delicate or antique fabrics.
